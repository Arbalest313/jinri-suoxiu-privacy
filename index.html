隐私说明

你写或说的话

你写或说的原话只在本次会话内存中。启用远程回话时，默认发给火山引擎方舟大模型生成贫道的回话；App 不保存原话，也不写入日志。识别到的危机内容不外发，改用本地固定回话。

语音与相机

语音只在本机离线识别，不录音存档。相机只在桌上起阵时使用；除非你点「AR 留影」，不会生成照片。

本机保存

阵的记录和回话保存在本机，不含你的原话；删除 App 可一并移除这些本机数据。道号与偏好设置也只存在这台设备。

你的选择

不需要账号，无广告、无追踪、无第三方统计。远程回话可在「回应设置」关闭；若更换模型服务商，原话交给你所选的服务商。服务商的数据处理以其政策为准。

Privacy Policy

Applies to 今日所修 and 今日所修·沉浸 v0.3.0. Updated October 17, 2026.

What you write or say

Your original words remain in memory for the current session. When remote replies are enabled, they are sent by default to Volcengine Ark models to generate Daoyou's reply. The App does not save or log your original words. Content detected as a crisis stays on the device and receives a fixed local response.

Speech and camera

Speech recognition runs offline on your device. The App does not archive audio recordings. The camera is used only when placing a formation on a table. No photo is generated unless you tap “AR 留影” (AR photo).

Data on your device

Formation records and replies are saved locally without your original words. Deleting the App also removes this local data. Your chosen name and preferences are stored only on this device.

Your choices

No account is required. The App has no ads, tracking, or third-party analytics. You can turn off remote replies in “回应设置” (reply settings). If you choose a different model provider, your words go to that provider. The provider's own policies govern its data processing.
